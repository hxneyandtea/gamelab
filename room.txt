Living Room
The small living room contains a couch, coffee table, and two
armchairs. Pictures of various cousins adorn the walls along
with two of your aunt's wretched attempts at oil painting. You
can't help but notice that the carpet could use a good
vacuuming.

Doorways lead west to your father's office, north to your bedroom, and east to the kitchen.
#

Bedroom
This is your domain and has been since your family moved here
when you were six. A nightstand with a Superman lamp sits next
to your twin bed. Posters of various anime characters cover one
wall. A pile of laundry, slowly but surely attaining sentience,
occupies the far corner.

The door to the attic is in the ceiling above the pile of clothes. You can return south to the living room.
#

Office
This is your father's office where he spends most of his days and
nights at. Around the room are clutters of papers, books, and miscellaneous items.
On your right is a large bookcase, and a few of the books are on the floor
beneath it. On your left is a window that spans the dark night outside.
In the middle is a desk with a single paper on it.

#

Kitchen
You're now in your kitchen-- one of your favorite places in the house. You can remember
your mom cooking you pancakes this morning, having overflowed your plate with syrup.
The fridge is slightly ajar, the light illuminating the area around it. In the sink are
stacks of dishes, and a plate is broken near the cabinet. There are many cupboards.

The only way is back is through the living room.
#

Attic
After climbing the ladder from your room, you find yourself in the attic. It's old and dusty and you can't rememeber
the last time you were in here. There's many boxes, some labeled with other rooms of the house, and others
peoples names such as "Mom" and "Dad." There's also some old furniture, toys, and even a creepy mannequin.

Below is the hatch back to your humble abode.

#

Garage
The garage contains an old beat up 2009 Toyota Camry, a tool rack, and a few cinderblocks and pieces of wood.
There isn't much in the room, but it's quite cold. You can recall during the summertime when your older brother
would work on the car while you sat and learned from him. A few of the tools are not in their normal spots.

You can either return to the Living Room or your Bedroom.
